Re: Use of the Interstate route marker to identify routes in the ExitAtlas app

Dear AASHTO,

I publish ExitAtlas, a road trip companion app for iPhone and the web. While a person drives an interstate, it shows the exits ahead, the services at each exit, and how many miles remain in each state.

To tell drivers which highway an exit or a distance refers to, the app draws a route marker in the shape of the Interstate shield with the route number (for example "95"), next to the exit or route it identifies. The marker is drawn by the app; it is not used in the app's name, icon, logo, advertising or App Store listing, and the app does not suggest any connection with or endorsement by AASHTO or any state department of transportation. Attached are screenshots showing every place the marker appears.

Before the app is offered in the App Store, I would like to confirm that this use, identifying actual Interstate routes as map and navigation products do, is acceptable to AASHTO, and to learn whether AASHTO requires a license or any notice for it. If AASHTO prefers that the app use a different marker, I would appreciate guidance on what is acceptable.
